📖 User's guide

Everything Lumen does and how to use it. Tap a topic to open it.

🚀 Getting started
  1. Lumen opens on your Learning Portal, a plain school-style page. Press your Study key (v) to go into Lumen, and press it again to go back to the portal.
  2. Set up your Learning Portal first. Go to Timetable, press + Add class for each lesson (day, start, end, subject), and add your own notes for each subject underneath. The portal then shows the notes for whatever class you have right now.
  3. Pick your IB programme in Settings → Learning Portal so the built-in notes match your level.
  4. Make an account (bottom-left, Sign in) to chat, add friends, post and use the Shop.
⌨️ Your three keys
  • Study (v): switches between Lumen and the Learning Portal. Closes any game or page you had open.
  • Swap (b): flips to the Learning Portal but keeps your game running (muted). Press again to carry on exactly where you were.
  • Panic (`): instantly leaves for your safe page (Google by default), or does whatever you chose in Panic button.
  • While typing in a chat box or post, a single press types the letter. Double-tap the key quickly to trigger it.
  • Hot corner: flick the mouse into the very top-left corner of the screen to jump to the Learning Portal. This works even inside websites in the Web tab, where browsers send key presses to the website.
  • Change any key in Settings → Keys & modes: click the box and press the new key.
⚡ Panic, disguise and history
  • Safe page: where Panic sends you. Disguise this visit in history renames Lumen's history entry (for example to "Google Classroom") before leaving.
  • Close the tab: browsers only let a site close tabs it opened itself. Use Stealth launch (Disguise card) once, and Panic can then close the tab.
  • Tab title and icon: make the tab look like Docs, Classroom or Google.
  • Lumen's Web tab doesn't add the pages you visit to your browser history. Only Lumen itself appears, listed as Learning Portal. Your network, school filter or a signed-in Google account can still keep their own records.
⏰ Timed cover pages and the Return-key extension
  • Pick times (for example a lesson) and a page. During those times, Panic and Swap show that page instead.
  • Essay, worksheet, Google, Wikipedia and Desmos show inside Lumen; press Panic again to come back.
  • Any other site (ManageBac, YouTube, Classroom…) can't be shown inside another site, so it opens for real in your tab. Come back with your browser's Back button, or install the Return-key extension (Settings) so your keys and hot corner bring you back from any site.
🏠 The Hub and 🛠 Lumen creators
  • Hub is the first page you see in Lumen: the admin's announcements, ⭐ Featured picks, the 🔥 most played games, the 📰 most popular posts this week and ✨ new projects from Lumen creators.
  • Each game or creator website counts one play per person per day.
  • Become a Lumen creator: press Apply at the bottom of the Hub and say what you'll make. Once the admin accepts you, a Creator page appears in the menu.
  • On the Creator page, add a game or a website (title, an https:// link, a short description and a cover image). The admin reviews it before it goes live; editing it sends it back for review. Your stats show plays, what's live and what's featured.
  • Approved games appear on the Hub and the Games tab; approved websites appear in Web → ⭐ Featured.
  • Creator shop items: on Games → Create, under 🎨 Shop creations, upload an animated GIF with a transparent background (up to 4 MB) or a photo (PNG, JPG or WebP up to 3 MB) to make a profile effect (plays over the big profile card), an avatar decoration (around the picture) or a nameplate. Then choose how it appears (Fill, Repeat or Place) and set its size, position and opacity with the sliders, the corner buttons, or by dragging it on the preview. Pick a name and price; once the admin approves it, it's sold in Shop → 🛠 Creator and you keep 90% of every sale. Creator items only appear in that tab.
  • Paid games: creators can set a price to unlock a game (0 = free) and keep 90% of every unlock. People unlock it once from the game's page; the link stays hidden until they do.
  • Editing: changing the name, description, price or placement of something that's already live updates it right away. A new picture, link or type goes back to the admin for review. Creators can wear their own approved shop items for free (👕 on Games → Create, or Equip (free) in the Shop).
  • Game covers can be up to 3 MB; Lumen shrinks them to fit, and small GIFs stay animated. After uploading, zoom and drag the cover to choose which part shows on the cards.
  • Creators earn Glow: ✦ 20 for every minute someone else plays your game inside Lumen (up to 4 hours per player per day), ✦ 50 for every post that mentions one of your games (one per post), and ✦ 50 for every positive review. Your own plays, posts and reviews don't count. Press Collect on Games → Create.
🎮 Games and 🌐 the Web tab
  • Games is the game hub: search, filter (Trending, Most played, New, Top rated, Creators, Lumen originals) and open any game to see its game page with stats and reviews. Press ▶ Play now to play. Your games at the bottom are games you added yourself (a link or an HTML file, kept in this browser only).
  • Nightbound lobbies: host a lobby in Nightbound inside Lumen and others see a Join button (on the Hub, the Game hub, Nightbound's page and, for friends, in the friends list). Pressing Join opens Nightbound and joins with the room code. Turn off Show the Nightbound lobbies I host to everyone in Settings to show them to friends only.
  • Browser size: in the Web tab, press ⤢. Stretch fills the whole window (Esc to go back), the sliders or the bottom-right corner change its size, and Minimize turns it into a small floating window you can drag around while you chat or play. Open the Web tab to bring it back.
  • Reviews: on a game page, pick 👍 or 👎 and write a few words. One review per person; you can edit your words later but not the thumbs.
  • Typing: when a friend is typing in your chat (or anyone in a channel), you'll see “… is typing” above the message box.
  • 🔔 Notifications: a pop-up and a red dot on the menu when someone messages you, likes your post, sends a friend request or when the admin gifts you Glow. Pop-ups never appear on the Learning Portal or a cover page, and you can turn them off in Settings.
  • Mention a game: write a game's name in a post and it turns into a 🎮 link to its game page.
  • Web: a browser inside Lumen with tabs. Searches use Google. ⭐ Featured in the toolbar lists websites made by Lumen creators. YouTube has its own page with Home, Shorts and search; videos play inside Lumen.
  • Many big sites (most Google pages, ManageBac, Instagram…) refuse to load inside other sites, both inside Lumen and in a cloaked tab. Press ⧉: sites that allow it open in a cloaked tab (not saved in your history); the rest open in a normal new tab.
💬 Chat, friends and posts
  • Chat: public channels everyone can read. Friends: search people, send requests, and chat privately. Only you two can read private chats.
  • Green dot = online, yellow = idle (Lumen in a background tab), grey = offline.
  • Formatting: **bold** (Ctrl+B), *italic* (Ctrl+I), __underline__ (Ctrl+U), ~~strike~~, `code` (Ctrl+E), ||spoiler||. Start a line with # for a big heading (##, ### smaller). 😊 adds emoji and GIF adds GIFs.
  • Messages: hover a message to react 😊, reply ↩, edit ✏️ (your own), copy or delete 🗑 it. Double-click a message to reply, press ↑ in an empty box to edit your last message, Esc to cancel. 📷 sends a picture (Lumen Lite).
  • Posts: share text and an image (small GIFs stay animated). People can like ❤️ and comment. Tap ⋯ on someone's post to hide posts from them (undo any time with 🙈 Muted at the top of Posts, in Profile → Privacy, or on their profile) or to report it. The Lumen team checks reports: if they agree, the post earns no Glow and its author is told why.
  • Daily challenges: right on the Hub (and at the top of Games): Minesweeper, Memory and Slide. Everyone gets the same puzzles each day; solve each one for ✦ 100 Glow (once a day, +5% with Lite).
  • Creators: make and publish games, websites and shop items in Games → Create. Games can be free or priced in ✦ Glow (you keep 90%), and approved games show up right on the Games page.
  • In games, double-tap your Study or Swap key to leave the game (a single press goes to the game). Panic is always one press.

🔑 Return-key extension

When you open a real site from Lumen (ManageBac, YouTube…), Lumen isn't running in that tab, and websites can't see key presses on other sites. This small Chrome extension listens for your Panic, Study and Swap keys (and the hot corner) on every site and takes you straight back to Lumen. It does nothing else.

⬇ Download extension
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the lumen-return-key folder.
  4. Reload Lumen once so it learns your keys.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. School-managed browsers may block extensions; your browser's Back button always works.
