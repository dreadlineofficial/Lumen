📖 User's guide

Everything Lumen does and how to use it. Tap a topic to open it.

🚀 Getting started
  1. Lumen opens on your Learning Portal, a plain school-style page. Press your Study key (v) to go into Lumen, and press it again to go back to the portal.
  2. Set up your Learning Portal first. Go to Timetable, press + Add class for each lesson (day, start, end, subject), and add your own notes for each subject underneath. The portal then shows the notes for whatever class you have right now.
  3. Pick your IB programme in Settings → Learning Portal so the built-in notes match your level.
  4. Make an account (bottom-left, Sign in) to chat, add friends, post and use the Shop.
⌨️ Your three keys
  • Study (v): switches between Lumen and the Learning Portal. Closes any game or page you had open.
  • Swap (b): flips to the Learning Portal but keeps your game running (muted). Press again to carry on exactly where you were.
  • Panic (`): instantly leaves for your safe page (Google by default), or does whatever you chose in Panic button.
  • While typing in a chat box or post, a single press types the letter. Double-tap the key quickly to trigger it.
  • Hot corner: flick the mouse into the very top-left corner of the screen to jump to the Learning Portal. This works even inside websites in the Web tab, where browsers send key presses to the website.
  • Change any key in Settings → Keys & modes: click the box and press the new key.
⚡ Panic, disguise and history
  • Safe page: where Panic sends you. Disguise this visit in history renames Lumen's history entry (for example to "Google Classroom") before leaving.
  • Close the tab: browsers only let a site close tabs it opened itself. Use Stealth launch (Disguise card) once, and Panic can then close the tab.
  • Tab title and icon: make the tab look like Docs, Classroom or Google.
  • Lumen's Web tab doesn't add the pages you visit to your browser history (only Lumen itself appears). Your network, school filter or a signed-in Google account can still keep their own records.
⏰ Timed cover pages and the Return-key extension
  • Pick times (for example a lesson) and a page. During those times, Panic and Swap show that page instead.
  • Essay, worksheet, Google, Wikipedia and Desmos show inside Lumen; press Panic again to come back.
  • Any other site (ManageBac, YouTube, Classroom…) can't be shown inside another site, so it opens for real in your tab. Come back with your browser's Back button, or install the Return-key extension (Settings) so your keys and hot corner bring you back from any site.
🎮 Games and 🌐 the Web tab
  • Games: click a card to play. Add a game takes a link or an HTML file from your device (files stay in this browser only).
  • Web: a browser inside Lumen with tabs. Searches use Google. YouTube has its own page with Home, Shorts and search; videos play inside Lumen.
  • Many big sites refuse to load inside other sites. If a page stays blank, press ⧉ Open cloaked.
💬 Chat, friends and posts
  • Chat: public channels everyone can read. Friends: search people, send requests, and chat privately. Only you two can read private chats.
  • Green dot = online, yellow = idle (Lumen in a background tab), grey = offline.
  • Formatting: **bold** (Ctrl+B), *italic* (Ctrl+I), __underline__ (Ctrl+U), ~~strike~~, `code` (Ctrl+E), ||spoiler||. Start a line with # for a big heading (##, ### smaller). 😊 adds emoji and GIF adds GIFs.
  • Posts: share text and an image. People can like ❤️ and comment.
✦ Glow, the Shop and your profile
  • Claim 500 Glow the first time and 150 Glow every day in the Shop.
  • Earn from posts: every view from someone else = 50 Glow (up to 2 per person per day), every comment from someone else = 150 Glow. Collect it in Posts → My analytics.
  • Spend it on profile effects, avatar decorations, nameplates, name effects and themes. Bought items appear in Owned; equip or unequip any time.
  • Profile: banner, picture, name style, status, about me, links, and what friends can see you doing.
🧩 Make it yours
  • Layout: menu on the left, right or top, icons only, compact spacing, text size, roundness, blur and background animation.
  • Export settings saves everything (including your timetable) to a file; Import restores it on another computer.

⚡ Panic button

Browsers only let a site close tabs that it opened itself. Use “Stealth launch” (Disguise card) so Close always works.

⌨ Keys & modes

Your keys work everywhere in Lumen. While typing, double-tap them. Inside a website opened in the Web tab, the browser sends key presses to that site, so use the hot corner there.

🎓 Learning Portal

Lumen always opens on your Learning Portal, which shows notes for the class you have right now. Fill it in: add your classes on the Timetable page, then add your own notes for each subject underneath.

🕶 Disguise

Cloaked tabs open as about:blank, so the address never shows in the address bar and isn't saved in your browser history. Panic can also close the tab.

🔑 Return-key extension

When you open a real site from Lumen (ManageBac, YouTube…), Lumen isn't running in that tab, and websites can't see key presses on other sites. This small Chrome extension listens for your Panic, Study and Swap keys (and the hot corner) on every site and takes you straight back to Lumen. It does nothing else.

⬇ Download extension
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the lumen-return-key folder.
  4. Reload Lumen once so it learns your keys.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. School-managed browsers may block extensions; your browser's Back button always works.

⏰ Timed cover pages

During these times, Panic and Swap show your chosen page inside Lumen instead. Press Panic again to come straight back. Tip: for Google Docs or Slides, use File → Share → Publish to web links; normal Google links refuse to load inside other sites.
